FAQ

Before you book.

Answers to the questions clients ask most before securing their date.

How do I book?

All inquiries and bookings run through Square. Start with the Book button, select the best option, and complete the requested details.

Do you require a retainer?

Yes. A retainer is used to secure your date and begin the booking process.

Do you offer photo and video?

Yes. KingVisual Studios offers photo-only, video-only, and combined coverage options.

How long does delivery take?

Photo delivery depends on the event. Wedding trailers are prioritized, while full cinematic films require a longer production timeline.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel coverage can be quoted based on location, hours, and production needs.
